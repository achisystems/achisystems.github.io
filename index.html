SEO Optimization

Search Engine Optimisation is the most cost-effective way to grow your business online over the long term. Unlike paid advertising that stops the moment you stop paying, good SEO builds compounding authority — ranking your website higher on Google month after month, bringing in qualified, free traffic.

At Achi Systems, our SEO approach starts with comprehensive keyword research. We identify the exact search terms your potential customers in Kenya are using — from high-volume head terms to valuable long-tail phrases — and build a content and optimisation strategy around them. We then carry out thorough on-page optimisation: title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, internal linking, image alt text, and schema markup.

Local SEO is a particular strength of ours. We optimise your Google Business Profile, build local citations across Kenyan directories, and create location-specific content that helps you dominate searches like "web design Nairobi" or "plumber Westlands." We also handle technical SEO — fixing crawl errors, improving page speed, implementing canonical tags, and ensuring your XML sitemap is up to date.

Every month, you receive a clear, jargon-free report showing your keyword rankings, organic traffic growth, and the work completed. Our goal is to make your website the most visible and trusted resource in your niche — bringing you leads and customers consistently, without the ongoing cost of paid ads.

Our maintenance packages cover all the essentials: regular CMS, plugin and theme updates to keep your software current and secure; weekly automated backups stored off-site so your data is always protected; uptime monitoring with instant alerts if your site goes down; and monthly performance audits to catch speed or SEO issues before they impact your business.
